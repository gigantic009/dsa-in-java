# Complete DSA Roadmap

## 00. Problem-solving foundations
- Read constraints, inputs, outputs and examples.
- Flowchart symbols: start/end, process, input/output, decision and arrows.
- Sequence, selection, iteration, pseudocode and dry-run tables.

## 01. Java foundations
- JDK/JRE/JVM, main method, variables, constants and scope.
- Primitive types (byte, short, int, long, float, double, char, boolean), reference types, String and arrays.
- Casting, type conversion, overflow, Scanner and output.
- Arithmetic, relational, logical, assignment, unary and bitwise operators.
- if/else, else-if, nested conditions, switch and ternary operator.
- for, while, do-while, nested loops, break and continue.
- Methods, parameters, return values, overloading and call stack.

## 02. Complexity analysis
- Operation counting; best, average and worst case.
- Big-O, Big-Ω and Big-Θ.
- O(1), O(log n), O(n), O(n log n), O(n²), O(2ⁿ), O(n!).
- Auxiliary space, recursion stack, in-place algorithms.
- Analyse loops, nested loops and recursive calls.

## 03. Arrays
- Traversal, update, insert/delete by shifting, min/max, reverse, rotate.
- Prefix/suffix sums, range queries, difference arrays.
- Two pointers, sliding window, subarrays and Kadane.
- Frequency counting, hashing, majority, missing/repeating, stock profit.

## 04. Searching & sorting
- Linear/binary search, first/last occurrence, bounds, rotated arrays, search on answer.
- Bubble, selection, insertion, counting, merge and quick sort.
- Stability, in-place property and complexity comparison.

## 05. 2D arrays
- Matrix declaration and nested traversal.
- Row/column/diagonal operations, transpose and rotation.
- Spiral traversal, matrix search, multiplication and prefix matrix.

## 06. Strings
- Immutability, StringBuilder, char arrays and character frequency.
- Reverse, palindrome, anagram, duplicate removal and rotations.
- Substrings, subsequences, common prefix and sliding-window strings.
- KMP, Rabin-Karp and Z algorithm.

## 07. Bit manipulation
- Binary representation; AND, OR, XOR, NOT and shifts.
- Check/set/clear/toggle bits, masks and XOR properties.
- Odd/even, power of two, count set bits and subset generation.

## 08. OOP
- Classes, objects, fields, methods, constructors, this and static.
- Encapsulation, access modifiers, inheritance and super.
- Overloading, overriding, runtime polymorphism.
- Abstract classes, interfaces and composition.

## 09. Recursion — two parts
### A. Fundamentals
- Base/recursive case, call stack, return flow and recursion tree.
- Print numbers, factorial, sum, Fibonacci, power.
- Array traversal, sorted check, first/last occurrence.
### B. Patterns
- Subsequences, subsets, permutations and combinations.
- Tiling/friends pairing, grid paths and maze.
- Choose-explore-unchoose, pruning and backtracking.

## 10. Divide & conquer
- Divide, solve, combine; recurrence relations and recursion trees.
- Binary search, merge sort, quick sort, inversion count and Master theorem.

## 11. Linked lists — two parts
### A. Singly linked list
- Nodes, traversal, insert/delete, search and length.
- Reverse, middle node, nth from end and cycle detection.
### B. Doubly/circular and interview patterns
- Doubly/circular operations, merge sorted lists, duplicates.
- Intersection, palindrome and reverse in k groups.

## 12. Stacks — two parts
### A. Fundamentals
- LIFO, array/list implementation, push/pop/peek.
- Balanced parentheses, expression conversion/evaluation.
### B. Monotonic stack
- Next greater/smaller, stock span, histogram rectangle and rain water.

## 13. Queues
- Array/list queue, circular queue, deque and PriorityQueue.
- Queue using stacks, stack using queues and sliding-window maximum.

## 14. Binary trees — three parts
### 1. Fundamentals
- Node, root/leaf, height/depth/level, count/sum/diameter.
### 2. Traversals
- Preorder, inorder, postorder, level order, iterative and zigzag.
- Left/right view, boundary and vertical order.
### 3. Tree patterns
- Balanced/identical/subtree, LCA, path sum.
- Build from traversals, serialize/deserialize and max path sum.

## 15. Binary search trees — two parts
### A. Operations
- Search, insert, delete, min/max, predecessor/successor.
### B. Advanced
- Validate BST, LCA, kth smallest/largest, balanced BST, recover swapped nodes.

## 16. Heaps & hashing
- HashMap/HashSet, frequency maps, collisions.
- Min/max heap, heapify, heap sort, top-K, kth largest and merge K lists.

## 17. Graphs — five parts
### 1. Representation
- Vertices/edges, directed/undirected, weighted/unweighted.
- Adjacency matrix/list and edge list.
### 2. Traversal
- BFS, DFS, components, grid traversal, flood fill and islands.
### 3. Cycles and ordering
- Cycle detection, bipartite, topological sort, Kahn's algorithm.
### 4. Shortest paths
- BFS shortest path, Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford and Floyd-Warshall.
### 5. MST and advanced
- DSU, path compression, union by rank/size, Kruskal and Prim.
- Bridges, articulation points and strongly connected components.

## 18. Greedy
- Greedy choice, activity/interval scheduling, fractional knapsack.
- Job sequencing, minimum platforms, gas station and Huffman overview.

## 19. Backtracking
- State-space tree, subsets, combinations, permutations.
- Combination sum, maze, Sudoku, N-Queens and pruning.

## 20. Dynamic programming — five parts
### 1. Foundations
- Overlapping subproblems, optimal substructure, memoization/tabulation.
### 2. 1D DP
- Fibonacci, stairs, min cost stairs, house robber, decode ways.
### 3. Grid DP
- Unique paths, obstacles, min path sum, triangle and falling path.
### 4. Subsequences/knapsack
- 0/1/unbounded knapsack, subset sum, partition, coin change, LIS, LCS.
### 5. Strings/interval/advanced
- Edit distance, palindromic subsequence, matrix-chain, stock DP, tree/bitmask DP.

## 21. Tries
- Node/children, insert/search/prefix, dictionary and autocomplete.

## 22. Segment trees
- Build, range sum/min/max, point update and lazy propagation.
- Fenwick tree comparison and coordinate compression.

## 23. Interview patterns
- Two pointers, sliding window, fast/slow, prefix sum + hashmap.
- Binary search on answer, monotonic stack/queue, top-K, intervals and DSU.

Difficulty levels are estimates for learning: Easy/Foundation, Medium/Intermediate and Hard/Advanced. Company tags must come from the supplied Apna sheet and are not guarantees of interview questions.
