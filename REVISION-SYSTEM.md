# Revision System

DSA mastery comes from solving again without looking at the answer.

## Suggested revisit schedule

For each problem you solve:
- **Day 0:** Solve, test and write the key idea.
- **Day 1:** Re-solve from a blank file without notes.
- **Day 7:** Re-solve or explain the algorithm and complexity.
- **Day 21:** Solve a variation or teach the concept aloud.
- **Day 45:** Mixed review under timed conditions.

Adjust the schedule if recall is easy or difficult.

## Mastery levels

- **Level 0 — Unseen:** not attempted.
- **Level 1 — Attempted:** tried independently, not solved.
- **Level 2 — Understood:** solution understood, but not independently reconstructed.
- **Level 3 — Reproduced:** solved independently with tests.
- **Level 4 — Mastered:** solved independently later and can explain/solve a variation.

Use honest levels; completion counts alone are not mastery.
