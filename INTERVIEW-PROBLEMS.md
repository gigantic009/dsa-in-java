## How to use this list
1. First learn the relevant subtopic theory.
2. Solve Easy/Foundation questions without looking at code.
3. Move to Medium/Intermediate, then Hard/Advanced.
4. Record your own attempt and complexity.
5. A company label is context for prioritisation, not a prediction of interview questions.

The full workbook remains the larger question bank; this page is a selected quick-practice shortlist, not the complete 731-question list.
