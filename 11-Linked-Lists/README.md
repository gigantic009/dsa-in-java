# Linked Lists

## Part A — Singly linked list
- Node, head, tail and traversal.
- Insert at head/tail/index; delete by position or key.
- Search, length and reverse iterative/recursive.
- Middle node, nth from end and Floyd cycle detection.

## Part B — Doubly, circular and interview patterns
- Doubly/circular list operations.
- Merge sorted lists, remove duplicates and reverse in k groups.
- Intersection, palindrome and flattening patterns.

## Practice
Reverse list; middle node; detect cycle; merge two sorted lists; remove nth from end; intersection; palindrome; reverse k-group.
